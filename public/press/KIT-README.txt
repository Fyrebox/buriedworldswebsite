GETTING THE HISTORY RIGHT
  Hoxne, 1992    Eric Lawes was searching for a hammer lost by his tenant
                 farmer, Peter Whatling, when he found the hoard. The hammer
                 was recovered too and is held with the hoard in the British
                 Museum, Room 49. The game recreates the hammer.
  Coloma, 1848   James Marshall's find at Sutter's Mill, January 1848,
                 started the California Gold Rush.
  Carcassonne    UNESCO-listed since 1997.
  Bolonia        The Roman town of Baelo Claudia, Andalusia.
  The detector   Gold and lead deliberately overlap on the target ID scale,
                 because that is what the ground does to you in reality.
